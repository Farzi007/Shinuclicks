About Me

Born and raised in Kerala, I fell in love with the art of photography while documenting my family's travels. I moved to Bangalore four years ago and the city's contradictions — its quiet gardens and roaring tech pulse — became my muse.

I shoot with natural light whenever possible. I believe the best photographs aren't taken — they're waited for. A patient eye is my most important piece of equipment.
